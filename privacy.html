§608 · Privacy Policy

608 Prep

Effective 26 November 2026

Support & FAQ Legal

608 Prep collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. We run no server for 608 Prep and receive no usage or study data from it.

Offline content inside the app

The 521 practice questions and explanations, twenty sections of U.S. federal regulations (40 CFR Parts 82 and 84 and 49 CFR 173), the glossary, key tables and refrigerant pressure-temperature tables are bundled in the app. Answering questions, searching the rule book and running the leak-rate, evacuation and pressure-temperature tools happen locally on your device.

Study history and preferences

608 Prep stores your study records on your iPhone so you can track weak topics and resume simulations:

  • answered questions: question identifier, section, topic, correctness, timestamp and mode (practice, daily mix or simulation);
  • spaced-review state and next review date for each question;
  • simulation runs: covered sections, start/finish time, recorded choices and per-section scores.

These records hold no name, email, location or contact information.

Private iCloud sync. If iCloud is enabled on your iPhone, iOS mirrors those study records into your own private CloudKit container (iCloud.app.klarwerk.prep608) so your other devices using the same Apple Account stay in sync. That private database belongs solely to your Apple Account; the developer cannot inspect or export it. With iCloud off, records remain solely on the device.

Device preferences. Local settings (onboarding state, sound and haptic toggles, target sections, exam date, daily target, reminder schedule, recent calculator inputs and cached Full Access status) stay on the device via Apple’s UserDefaults API. The app’s privacy manifest declares this on-device preference usage (CA92.1) with zero tracking domains and zero collected data types.

Daily reminder

The daily study reminder is disabled by default. Turning it on in Plan asks iOS for local notification permission and schedules alerts strictly on the device.

In-app purchase

Full Access (app.klarwerk.prep608.pro) is a one-time non-consumable purchase handled exclusively by Apple StoreKit. The developer never sees your payment card or Apple Account credentials.

Network activity

608 Prep connects to the network only when:

  • communicating with Apple’s App Store to fetch the localized price, complete a purchase or restore Full Access;
  • syncing study records with your private iCloud container when iCloud is enabled;
  • opening an external reference on ecfr.gov, epa.gov or this policy page in an in-app browser when you tap a link.

Email you choose to send

Report a mistake in this question and Email support open a draft in your own mail app. Nothing is transmitted unless you press Send. If you email us, we use your message and return address solely to respond and fix verified question errors.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Reset all progress permanently deletes your practice history and simulation records on the device and in your private iCloud container.
  • Removing the app deletes local data and preferences; iCloud storage can also be managed from iOS Settings under your Apple Account.
